Studio notes

Workflow

  1. Load a clean 4–12 s voice clip and type its exact transcript.
  2. Paste the speech. Use blank lines for paragraphs.
  3. Build chunks, review the split, then Generate all. Long scripts run chunk by chunk.
  4. Assemble final applies gaps, pitch and speed. Download WAV or MP3.

Markup

[pause=1.5s]Insert silence after the sentence. Also accepts [pause=750ms] or <break time="2s"/>.
*words* / **words**Mark the sentence as emphasized: slightly slower native delivery and extra gain at assembly.
Blank lineParagraph break with the paragraph gap.

Good to know

  • F5-TTS clips reference audio to 12 seconds. Trim to the strongest, cleanest line.
  • Chunks are generated sequentially. A chunk can be edited, regenerated, muted, or assigned its own pause.
  • Native speed, NFE, CFG, sway, seed and RMS are baked into a take. Master speed and pitch are applied later.
  • ROCm (AMD Radeon) is Linux-only. Install with studio/install.sh --rocm and the studio uses the GPU automatically; force it with F5_TTS_DEVICE=rocm or fall back with F5_TTS_DEVICE=cpu. On Windows the studio runs on CPU.
  • Shift-click a chunk's regenerate button to keep its seed; normal click produces a new take.
  • Shortcuts: Ctrl/Cmd+Enter generate, Ctrl/Cmd+S assemble, Esc stop playback.
